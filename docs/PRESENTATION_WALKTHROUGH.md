## Email and in-app notifications

Setup briefings are in-app only; no new bulk setup email was sent. Normal actions such as task assignment, feedback, submission, review invitations and editorial decisions create real notification jobs.

For workflow email, the recipient must have a verified email address and email notifications enabled in Account settings. Complete verification on the presenter computer before class; localhost links are not hosted public links. The server must remain running. A successful SMTP handoff is not proof that a message reached the inbox; check Gmail and spam as well as the Administrator's delivery queue. You can demonstrate in-app notifications even if email has not been verified.
