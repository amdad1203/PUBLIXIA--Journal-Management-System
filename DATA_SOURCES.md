Sources: [OpenAlex API](https://help.openalex.org/api/), [Crossref REST API](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/), [Europe PMC REST API](https://europepmc.org/RestfulWebService), [DataCite API](https://support.datacite.org/docs/api), [TOPSCINET Bangladesh](https://topscinet.com/rankings/bgd/), [Scientific Bangladesh report](https://scientificbangladesh.com/top-10-of-top-2-scientists-from-bangladesh-in-2026/).

TOPSCINET entries were transcribed from its public source table; their composite score excludes self-citations according to that source. The ranking type is not independently inferred as career-long or single-year. Scientific Bangladesh is a linked contextual report, not an API integration. No ranking identities were automatically merged into OpenAlex identities.

Existing fictional workflow records remain labeled Practice record. Wording changes are backed up in contentrevisionbackups. Internal provenance and mail-suppression flags remain intact. The 406-page user-supplied paper compilation informed cybersecurity queries, including SecureBERT and cyber threat intelligence; its full text is not served by the app.
